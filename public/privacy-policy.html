Privacy Policy

Wren & Martin Grammar Master

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wren & Martin Grammar Master (“the app”) handles information when you use the app.

Information stored by the app

The app stores learning progress and related preferences locally on your device so that lessons, quiz progress and certificates can work. The app does not require you to create an account, and the developer does not operate an app account system or cloud-sync service.

Advertising and third-party services

The app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, diagnostics, and ad interactions to provide, measure, and improve advertising, subject to Google's policies and your device and consent settings. Where required, the app requests consent using Google's consent tools. You can review Google's information about how it handles data at Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

The developer does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by Google as part of providing advertising and related services, as described in Google's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

The app is an educational grammar-learning tool. It is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through accounts or forms. Advertising availability and settings are subject to applicable laws and Google Play and AdMob requirements.

Data retention and deletion

Learning progress stored locally remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Advertising-related data handled by Google is subject to Google's retention and deletion practices.

Security

We take reasonable care in designing the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be available at this page.
