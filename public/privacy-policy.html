Privacy Policy

Wren Martin Grammar Master

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Wren Martin Grammar Master (“the app”) handles information. The app is an English grammar learning tool with Urdu explanations, lessons, quizzes, practice and local learning progress.

Accounts and information you provide

The app does not require an account, password, phone number or email address to use its learning features. The developer does not operate an account system or cloud-sync service.

Learning progress, such as completed lessons, quiz statistics, practice dates and an unfinished exam session, is saved locally on your device so the app can restore your progress. This information is not sent to the developer by the app. It remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

If you enter a name for a course certificate, the name is used in the certificate during that session and is not included in the app's saved learning-progress record. The certificate PDF is generated on your device. The app opens the device sharing interface only when you choose to save or share the certificate; the destination you select then handles the file under its own privacy terms.

Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

On supported Android devices, the app may display banner advertisements using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information needed to provide and measure ads, which can include an advertising or device identifier, IP address, device and app information, diagnostics, and ad interactions. Google handles this information under its own policies and settings.

The app requests advertising consent through Google's consent tools where supported and required. You can read more in the Google Privacy Policy and Google's information about AdMob and data.

To keep the learning app usable on devices that commonly do not include Google Play services, Google Mobile Ads initialization is skipped on Huawei-branded Android devices. Ads are optional; failure to load an ad does not prevent access to lessons, quizzes, progress or certificates.

Sharing and sale of information

The developer does not sell personal information. The app does not transmit learning progress to the developer. Information processed by Google for advertising, or by an app you choose in the device sharing interface, is handled by those third parties under their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

The app does not ask learners to create accounts or submit personal details to use its learning features. Advertising services may apply their own age, consent and regional requirements. Parents or guardians should supervise use of the device's advertising and privacy settings where appropriate.

Security and retention

Learning progress is stored in the app's private local storage. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes that local progress. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features, third-party services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be available at this page.
